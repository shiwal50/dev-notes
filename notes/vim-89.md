## Summary

The mental model matters more than memorizing the API. Think of vim as a state machine with well-defined transitions, and most of the edge cases become obvious.

## Key points

- vim and curl are often used together, but they solve different problems
- The ecosystem is mature - prefer well-maintained libraries over rolling your own
- Community plugins vary wildly in quality - check maintenance status before depending on them
